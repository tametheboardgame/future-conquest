# R3-WP3.8A Approved Visual References

Status: APPROVED DIRECTION  
Package: R3-WP3.8A  
Review date: 15 August 2026

The visual review approved a landmark-first miniature direction for all three Pass 1 cities. The approved generated renders are the visual target for proportion, hierarchy and recognisability. Runtime implementation remains procedural Three.js geometry rather than baked image assets.

## London

Primary read: Elizabeth Tower / Big Ben.

Approved characteristics:
- the tower occupies the overwhelming majority of the vertical silhouette;
- the clock stage is large, square and unmistakable;
- all four clock faces are part of the model language;
- the belfry, steep roof and narrow spire create the correct upper silhouette;
- the Palace of Westminster is visibly subordinate and functions as a low architectural base.

## Paris

Primary read: Eiffel Tower.

Approved characteristics:
- four broad flared feet define the base;
- the structure visibly narrows through successive observation levels;
- open lattice/bracing is fundamental to the silhouette;
- the first, second and top deck hierarchy is readable;
- the Arc de Triomphe may appear only as a small secondary landmark.

## Brussels

Primary read: Atomium.

Approved characteristics:
- the metallic spheres and tubular connections dominate the entire composition;
- the geometry must read as the Atomium rather than an arbitrary molecule;
- the nine-sphere body-centred cubic structure is the runtime construction basis;
- support legs and the upper antenna reinforce the real landmark silhouette;
- Brussels Town Hall is secondary and substantially smaller.

## Pass 1 acceptance principle

A player should be able to identify London, Paris or Brussels from the physical landmark miniature before reading the city label. Later WP3.8 passes should follow this same principle.

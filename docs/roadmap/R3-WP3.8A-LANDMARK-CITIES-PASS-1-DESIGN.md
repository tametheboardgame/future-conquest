# R3-WP3.8A Landmark Cities Pass 1 - Asset-Driven Model Design Lock

Status: IMPLEMENTATION ACTIVE / V2 REWORK  
Package: R3-WP3.8A  
Cities: London, Paris, Brussels  
Baseline: accepted R3-WP3.7 `main`

## Why WP3.8A changed

The first procedural implementation proved the geographic, LOD and performance architecture but failed the product-owner visual bar. More boxes, cylinders and cones made the landmarks more accurate without producing the detailed premium board-game-piece quality established by the approved concept renders.

WP3.8A is therefore reworked around **authored self-hosted 3D miniature assets**. The approved board-game-piece renders are now the visual specification rather than loose inspiration.

A first live review of the London V2 asset also established that restricting authored landmarks to Selected/local zoom hides the art during the normal Campaign command-map view. Approved authored city pieces must therefore appear in Campaign as well as Selected/local whenever they are visible; only Theatre retains the cheap fallback.

## Canonical visual targets

- London: ornate Elizabeth Tower / Big Ben and Palace of Westminster, with strong Gothic relief, clock detail, pinnacles, deliberate miniature base and restrained supporting scenery.
- Paris: detailed Eiffel Tower with dense readable latticework and observation decks, with the Arc de Triomphe subordinate on the same deliberate diorama base.
- Brussels: polished Atomium with prominent metallic spheres, complete tube structure and supports, with a subordinate Town Hall/Gothic spire and deliberate miniature base.

Recognition remains important, but Campaign and Selected/local quality must communicate **crafted miniature detail**. The target is closer to a premium strategy-board sculpt than to a low-poly map icon.

## Runtime architecture lock

- The authoritative `STRATEGIC_NODES` coordinates remain the only geographic anchors.
- MapLibre terrain elevation and the existing 22 m world-layer clearance remain unchanged.
- The authored model is a child visual of the existing strategic-node root. It never owns longitude, latitude, territory control, collision, movement, route or save-state authority.
- Authored Campaign/Selected assets use self-hosted glTF/GLB-compatible delivery through the normal game build. No third-party runtime model hosting is permitted.
- Asset source is committed as deterministic authored geometry/build input and emitted during `npm run build` into ordinary files under `public/miniatures/wp3-8a/`.
- Each generated asset receives build-time format validation and a SHA-256 manifest entry.
- Detailed authored models are lazy-loaded when their city is visible in Campaign or Selected/local view.
- Theatre retains a cheap procedural fallback representation.
- Loading failure also retains the procedural fallback, so landmark art can never make the command map unusable.
- Existing viewport culling remains mandatory. Off-screen detailed city models must not trigger unnecessary terrain work or rendering cost.
- Existing city/hub Layers controls remain authoritative for presentation visibility.

## Staged rollout

### Stage 1 - London end-to-end proof

London is the proven runtime-authored reference implementation. Its asset is generated by the build, loaded through Three.js `GLTFLoader`, attached beneath the London strategic-node root, and identified in browser evidence as `authored-gltf` in both Campaign and Selected/local view.

The current London asset is approximately 3.6k authored faces and includes four clock faces and hands, Gothic ribs/buttresses, roof/spire/weather-vane, Westminster massing, repeated windows/pinnacles, supporting trees and a tiered game-piece base.

### Stage 2 - Paris authored miniature

Paris now follows the same asset path. The generated self-hosted glTF is approximately 4.8k authored faces. Its Eiffel Tower uses four flared converging legs, dense crossed lattice bracing across multiple vertical bands, three observation/deck stages, upper shaft and mast. The Arc de Triomphe and a small amount of supporting Parisian scenery remain subordinate so the Eiffel silhouette dominates at Campaign scale.

### Stage 3 - Brussels authored miniature

Brussels now follows the same asset path. The generated self-hosted glTF is approximately 4.5k authored faces. The Atomium uses nine prominent metallic spheres in the body-centred-cubic arrangement, all cube-edge tubes and centre-to-corner connections, plus structural supports. The Town Hall/Gothic spire and supporting scenery remain subordinate so the Atomium remains the primary read.

WP3.8A is not complete until all three authored assets have passed browser/performance validation and received product-owner visual acceptance in the deployed game.

## LOD rules

Theatre:
- cheap procedural city silhouette only;
- existing strategic-node importance rules continue to decide visibility;
- no authored high-detail asset fetch.

Campaign:
- if an approved authored asset is available and the city is in the viewport, begin lazy loading;
- retain the procedural fallback while loading;
- swap to the authored glTF after a successful load;
- preserve viewport culling so only visible authored cities incur runtime cost.

Selected/local:
- continue using the same loaded authored asset;
- retain the procedural fallback while loading or after load failure;
- revert/retain fallback on failure without affecting the map or campaign;
- cull the complete strategic-node root when outside the padded viewport.

## Performance and provenance boundary

The detailed miniature assets are self-created for Future Conquest from the approved project visual direction. Third-party game meshes are not copied and external runtime model/CDN dependencies are not introduced.

Higher geometric detail is permitted behind the lazy Campaign/Selected viewport boundary. Exact-head browser and terrain-performance evidence remains mandatory. A visual improvement is not accepted by weakening the existing performance budget.

## Acceptance criteria

WP3.8A V2 completes only when:

- London, Paris and Brussels visibly approach the approved premium board-game-piece renders rather than the old primitive landmark approximations;
- each city uses an authored self-hosted asset in the normal Campaign view and Selected/local view;
- Theatre remains inexpensive and readable;
- loading/error fallback is deterministic and usable;
- the authoritative `STRATEGIC_NODES` coordinates, terrain grounding and 22 m clearance are unchanged;
- no gameplay, balance, save, route, territory or hidden-information authority changes;
- exact-head production build, dedicated landmark browser gate, general terrain visual gate and performance gate are green;
- final deployed visual evidence is accepted by the product owner.

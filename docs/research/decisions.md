# Design Decision Register

## D-001: Present-day setting

- Status: Approved
- Decision: Campaigns use versioned World States representing the contemporary date.
- Reason: Current alliances and conflicts are central to replayability, but genuinely live operational data is neither reliable nor appropriate.

## D-002: Deliberate temporal intervention

- Status: Approved
- Decision: The general intentionally enters a temporal distortion to alter the political development of 21st-century Europe.
- Reason: This provides a coherent player identity and motivation.

## D-003: Predestination ending

- Status: Approved
- Decision: The invasion creates the conditions that lead to the future war.
- Reason: It gives the campaign a strong narrative reversal while allowing the player's conduct to shape the resulting future.

## D-004: Turn-based campaign

- Status: Approved
- Decision: The campaign is turn-based, with each turn representing approximately one day.
- Reason: The game requires simultaneous management of formations, supply, diplomacy, occupation and escalation.

## D-005: Standard map first

- Status: Approved
- Decision: Design the approximately 100-territory Standard map first. Derive Rapid and Grand modes through stable merge and split relationships.
- Reason: This avoids maintaining three unrelated maps.

## D-006: Hybrid strategic boundaries

- Status: Approved
- Decision: Use recognisable administrative boundaries where possible, but prioritise military and logistical geography.
- Reason: No single European administrative classification covers the whole theatre or produces consistently playable territories.

## D-007: Separate geometry, claim and control

- Status: Approved
- Decision: Permanent territory geometry is independent from political claimant and actual controller.
- Reason: World States must represent changing conflicts and disputed areas without redrawing the campaign map.

## D-008: Abstract military locations

- Status: Approved
- Decision: Do not reproduce exact operational military locations. Generate deployments within broad authored military regions.
- Reason: Public data is incomplete, mutable and unsuited to a balanced game.

## D-009: Microstates as nodes

- Status: Provisional
- Decision: Andorra, Liechtenstein, Monaco, San Marino and Vatican City are political nodes rather than conventional territories.
- Reason: Their polygons would be difficult to select and would add little military gameplay.

## D-010: Europe as a bounded theatre

- Status: Provisional
- Decision: Include European Russia and European Türkiye while treating Asian Russia and Anatolia as off-map theatres.
- Reason: The campaign needs meaningful eastern edges without requiring conquest of all transcontinental states.

## D-011: Serious tone

- Status: Approved
- Decision: Use serious speculative military science fiction with limited dark humour.
- Reason: Logistics, occupation and civilian consequences are central to the concept.

# Motion-comic artwork prompts v1.0

This document supersedes `motion-comic-art-prompts-v0.1.md`. All panels inherit the approved canon in `../art/visual-design-bible-v1.md` and `../art/canon-generation-prompt-v1.md`.

## Shared production requirements

- Painted military graphic-novel illustration, 16:9
- Use the approved character and environment references
- No embedded text, captions, logos or watermarks
- Reserve safe negative space for caption overlays
- Keep focal subjects clear on desktop and mobile crops
- Produce background, midground, foreground and effects separation where practical
- The selected General variant is substituted consistently throughout one playthrough
- Exact portal arrival location remains visually non-specific

## 01: The world that remains

A shattered but inhabited future European industrial city at dawn. Flooded transport corridors, damaged historical and modern architecture, smoke, emergency repairs and distant defensive activity. One canon powered infantry operator crosses the foreground carrying the approved compact energy rifle. The soldier is exhausted and alert, not triumphant. Ash, rain and practical cold light. Show evidence that civilisation is still functioning under collapse.

## 02: The final command

A damaged subterranean command centre running on constrained power. The selected General stands fully armoured before a fractured tactical projection of Europe. The functional command overlayer, backpack interface and modular armour are visible. Staff officers work around repaired consoles and casualty projections. Authority comes from composure and competence, not ceremony.

## 03: The anomaly

Future analysts isolate corrupted temporal evidence in a functional military intelligence laboratory. A visual connection forms between the ruined future and present-day Europe, but the target remains incomplete and uncertain. Equipment is modular, repaired and power-conscious. Avoid mystical imagery.

## 04: The hypothesis

A split composition contrasting exhausted future Europe with ordinary present-day European streets, railways and public infrastructure. Branching causal paths converge on an unidentified event without revealing it. The future is colder and depleted; the present is warmer and mundane.

## 05: The order

The selected fully armoured General addresses massed canon infantry in a vast deployment facility assembled from repaired industrial infrastructure. Armour across the ranks shows standardised modules, replacement plates and varied maintenance history. No parade-ground decoration. Sequential practical lights and sparse cyan optics reveal the scale of the irreversible expedition.

## 06: The portal

A violent temporal rupture forms inside a ruined future industrial complex during heavy rain. Use an impossible dark centre, bent geometry, pale cyan spatial distortion and limited violet-white instability. Debris is pulled inward before the pressure wave. Canon infantry brace and advance using disciplined procedures.

## 07: Arrival

The portal erupts beside a location-neutral present-day European transport corridor. Contemporary civilians, police, emergency services and military responders react rationally. Future infantry establish a cautious perimeter without celebratory or villainous posing. Modern infrastructure and clothing must be realistic.

## 08: First response

A controlled graphic-novel montage of military mobilisation, emergency broadcasts, satellite observation, government crisis rooms and public footage. Present-day institutions are confused but competent. Future troops appear as a severe unknown threat, not as monsters.

## 09: The burden of command

Inside a field command platform, the selected General and command staff face an operational map of Europe. Their equipment follows the canon command-armour system. Present-day city lights and the portal perimeter are visible beyond armoured glass. The composition frames conquest as a grim calculation.

## 10: Title plate

A dark operational map of Europe with one portal event at an unspecified initial territory. Strategic routes and uncertain contacts fade into black. Portal violet-white remains the only strong chromatic event. Reserve central negative space for the Future Conquest title added by the application.

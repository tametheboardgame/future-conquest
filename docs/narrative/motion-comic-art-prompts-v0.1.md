# Motion-comic artwork prompt sheet v0.1

## Shared visual language

Use this direction for every panel:

`Cinematic military graphic novel, painterly realism, grounded science fiction, wide 16:9 composition, strong foreground silhouettes, deep atmospheric perspective, restrained palette, tactile weather and debris, dramatic practical lighting, detailed but readable at game-screen size, no text, no logos, no watermarks, no recognisable real politicians or military leaders.`

Future scenes use cold blue-grey, ash black and dirty white light. Portal energy uses violet-white with pale cyan edges. Present-day scenes introduce warmer sodium street lighting and recognisable civilian infrastructure.

## Character continuity

### The General

- Older military commander, gender presentation deliberately non-specific
- Close-cropped grey hair visible only in partial profile
- Long dark command coat over worn tactical armour
- No medals or national insignia
- Face usually obscured by shadow, holographic light or rear framing
- Calm, exhausted and resolute rather than theatrical

### Future infantry

- Human proportions, not oversized robots
- Scarred powered armour with field repairs, mismatched plates and exposed service cabling
- Narrow horizontal cyan helmet optics
- Compact energy rifles with physical magazines or power cells
- No capes, giant shoulder armour or superhero poses
- Equipment should look finite, maintained and increasingly difficult to replace

### Portal

- Circular spatial rupture rather than a manufactured ring
- Violet-white centre with pale cyan filaments
- Distorts rain, dust, light and nearby straight lines
- Pulls small debris inward before pushing a pressure wave outward

## Panel prompts

### 01: The world that remains

`Shattered future European city at dawn after decades of war, flooded avenue reflecting burning tower blocks, broken rail viaduct and collapsed modern architecture, drifting ash, distant weapons fire, one scarred powered infantry soldier crossing the foreground with damaged compact energy rifle, cyan visor dim and intermittent, exhausted scale rather than heroic pose, cinematic military graphic novel, painterly realism, cold desaturated blue-grey, wide 16:9, no text.`

Layer priorities: ruined skyline, floodwater and smoke, foreground soldier, ash and distant fire effects.

### 02: The final command

`Subterranean future command centre carved into damaged concrete, older General seen from behind in long dark command coat and worn tactical armour, fractured holographic map of Europe floating above a command table, staff officers in silhouette, casualty projections and failing sectors represented by abstract red tactical shapes, cold white overhead light, cyan holographic glow, restrained military graphic novel realism, wide 16:9, no readable text.`

Layer priorities: command chamber, map table, General silhouette, hologram and dust.

### 03: The anomaly

`Future intelligence laboratory under emergency power, scientists and military analysts surrounding suspended fragments of corrupted archival data, one luminous temporal line connecting a ruined future Europe map to a clean present-day Europe map, target point partly obscured by damaged data, glass screens, scanning light and cable bundles, grounded science fiction, painterly military graphic novel, wide 16:9, no readable text.`

Layer priorities: analysts, data fragments, temporal line, scan effects.

### 04: The hypothesis

`Split cinematic composition showing devastated future Europe on the left and peaceful present-day European city streets, railway and motorway on the right, both connected by branching translucent timelines converging on one unidentified glowing event, visual uncertainty and moral tension, no obvious villain, painterly military graphic novel realism, cold future versus warmer present, wide 16:9, no text.`

Layer priorities: two time periods, timeline branches, unidentified catalyst glow.

### 05: The order

`Vast underground deployment bay, older General on elevated platform addressing rows of one hundred thousand future infantry disappearing into darkness, soldiers in scarred field-repaired powered armour with mismatched plates and compact energy rifles, hangar lights activating in sequence, cyan helmet optics appearing across the formation, sombre irreversible mobilisation, grounded military science fiction graphic novel, wide 16:9, no text.`

Layer priorities: bay architecture, massed ranks, General platform, sequential lights.

### 06: The portal

`Ruined future industrial complex in heavy rain as a colossal circular spatial rupture opens in mid-air, violet-white portal with pale cyan filaments distorting steel beams and rain, debris lifting inward, future infantry bracing and advancing into the light, powered armour scarred and practical, intense pressure and scale, cinematic painterly military graphic novel, wide 16:9, no text.`

Layer priorities: industrial ruins, portal, first infantry wave, rain and debris.

### 07: Arrival

`Present-day European transport corridor beside railway lines and motorway interchange at dusk, violet-white temporal portal erupting into the scene, civilians and vehicles fleeing, emergency services converging, future infantry establishing a disciplined perimeter without firing, modern infrastructure recognisable but not tied to one exact country, tense first contact, grounded painterly military graphic novel, wide 16:9, no text.`

Layer priorities: transport corridor, portal, civilians and emergency response, perimeter troops.

### 08: First response

`Single graphic-novel montage composition divided by hard diagonal panels: European military units mobilising, fighter aircraft and helicopters launching, emergency television studio, satellite tracking display, government crisis room, police cordons and public phone footage, future infantry shown only as uncertain silhouettes, rational defensive response rather than villainy, painterly military realism, wide 16:9, no readable text.`

Layer priorities: montage frames, alert lighting, movement arrows and broadcast interference.

### 09: The burden of command

`Interior of a future field command vehicle overlooking the portal perimeter, viewpoint behind an unnamed operational commander facing a holographic tactical map of Europe divided into campaign territories, task-group symbols and escalation indicators, distant present-day city lights beyond armoured glass, conquest framed as a grim calculation rather than triumph, cinematic painterly military graphic novel, wide 16:9, no readable text.`

Layer priorities: field command interior, player silhouette, tactical Europe map, portal beyond glass.

### 10: Title plate

`Dark tactical map of Europe viewed from above, one violet-white portal burning at the first controlled territory, faint strategic routes and military contact markers, edges of the continent disappearing into black, ominous uncertainty and restrained scale, clean central negative space reserved for game title to be added separately, painterly military strategy game key art, wide 16:9, no text, no logos.`

Layer priorities: Europe map, portal point, tactical overlays, central title space.

## Negative direction

Avoid:

- Shiny pristine armour
- Superhero poses
- Space opera ships or alien armies
- National flags dominating compositions
- Readable invented interface text
- Excessive neon cyberpunk colour
- Photorealistic celebrity faces
- Gore or graphic civilian casualties
- Images presenting present-day European defenders as evil
- Visual confirmation of what the catalyst is

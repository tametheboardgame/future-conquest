# Operational combat findings v0.1

## Test coverage

Six representative multi-day operations were each run 1,500 times, producing
9,000 seeded operations. All six validation checks pass.

## Results

### Rapid security seizure

The security force withdraws after a median of two days. The future task group
has approximately five combat casualties and converts a small number of suits
from functional to damaged or broken states.

### Reinforced mechanised breakthrough

A second future task group arrives on day three. The objective is captured in
99.5 per cent of runs, normally on day three. The combined future force suffers
approximately 44 combat casualties and finishes with roughly 728 damaged and 93
unarmoured soldiers. Victory therefore consumes meaningful advanced capability.

Median medical and recovery outcomes include nine killed, seven critically
wounded, 29 recoverable wounded and 79 recovered broken suits. Fourteen suits
and seven energy weapons are exposed to enemy capture.

### Cautious probe against combined arms

The future task group is repulsed after two days but preserves approximately
2,414 active personnel. An orderly single-route withdrawal still exposes around
35 suits and 44 energy weapons, demonstrating that a sensible retreat limits
casualties without eliminating technology risk.

### Reinforced future defence

The 15,000-person combined-arms attack normally takes the position in four days,
even after 2,500 future reinforcements arrive. Modern forces suffer around 799
combat casualties; the future defenders suffer around 389.

The future force retains substantial combat strength but loses battlefield
ownership. Median exposure reaches approximately 188 suits and 215 energy
weapons. This is a strategic defeat even though the formation survives.

### Degraded urban offensive

The future task group withdraws after two days in every baseline run. It retains
most personnel, but its armour state is insufficient to overcome supported urban
infantry. The system therefore discourages treating depleted formations as
interchangeable with fresh task groups.

### Encircled future defence

The position falls after three days. The future force suffers approximately
1,140 combat casualties, including median aftermath estimates of 479 killed,
205 critically wounded, 228 recoverable wounded and 228 captured.

Only around five broken suits are recovered; approximately 87 suits and 1,109
energy weapons are exposed to capture. This produces the greatest technology
escalation in the test and establishes encirclement as a potential campaign
catastrophe.

## Decision

Multi-day combat now creates operational delay, persistent depletion and
meaningful reasons to reinforce or withdraw. The model is ready to be connected
to the full campaign simulation.

The next package should combine portal location, territory expansion, active
battles, garrisons, medical returns and armour repair across an entire campaign.

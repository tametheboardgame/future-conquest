# Combat balance findings v0.1

## Test

Seven representative engagements were each resolved 2,000 times, producing
14,000 seeded balance runs. The validation checks all pass.

## Key medians

- Future task group versus security force: 3 future casualties, 13 damaged
  suits and decisive victory.
- Future task group versus mechanised brigade: 18 future casualties, 79 damaged
  suits and victory.
- Future task group versus combined arms with air support: 41 future casualties,
  108 damaged suits and decisive defeat.
- Depleted future task group versus mechanised brigade: 48 future casualties,
  63 further damaged suits and defeat.
- Predominantly unarmoured future task group attacking supported urban infantry:
  70 future casualties and decisive defeat.
- 5,000 future defenders against 15,000 combined-arms attackers: the modern
  force normally takes the position, but loses approximately 202 personnel while
  inflicting 71 future casualties and damaging 232 suits.

## Interpretation

The advantage of the future army is concentrated in intact powered armour rather
than an unexplained universal combat bonus. Heavy modern forces can convert a
battlefield defeat into permanent strategic damage by degrading suits. Repeated
victories can therefore still move the player towards eventual defeat.

The initial values are suitable for multi-day operational testing. They must not
be treated as final casualty rates until retreat, medical recovery, battlefield
salvage and reinforcement are included.

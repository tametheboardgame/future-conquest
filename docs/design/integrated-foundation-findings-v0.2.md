# Integrated foundation findings v0.2

## Test envelope

The final integration ran 3,636 campaigns:

- all 101 portal territories
- three deterministic seeds per start
- all four army settings
- restrained, pragmatic and coercive occupation strategies
- up to 500 campaign days

Every run included persistent future task groups, independent contemporary brigades, multi-day battles, medical returns, armour damage, equipment loss, supply, occupation, resistance, escalation, intervention and strategic risk.

## Restrained baseline

The restrained strategy is the authoritative balance baseline because it represents competent play that protects the coalition needed for conquest.

- Campaign completes in a median 94 turns, with 96.4 per cent of runs inside the 60 to 100 target.
- Standard completes in a median 123 turns, with every run inside the 90 to 150 target.
- Veteran completes in a median 170 turns, with every run inside the 140 to 220 target.
- Desperate completes in a median 351 turns, with 94.7 per cent inside the 220 to 365 target.

All 1,212 restrained campaigns complete. Portal location still affects the edges of Campaign and Desperate timing.

## Coalition transition

Modern formations lead more territorial victories as the initial future force shrinks:

- Campaign: median 74 modern-led victories
- Standard: median 81
- Veteran: median 87
- Desperate: median 93

This is the strongest confirmation of the intended mode structure. Future troops act increasingly as command, breakthrough and specialist forces while the contemporary coalition carries the conventional war.

Median modern deaths also rise from roughly 1,650 in Campaign to 3,500 in Desperate. Delegating combat preserves future troops but does not make conquest bloodless.

## Future depletion

Restrained medians leave approximately:

- 90,200 functional suits in Campaign
- 45,500 in Standard
- 22,700 in Veteran
- 9,100 in Desperate

The smaller modes lose fewer future personnel because reliable modern forces assume a greater share of combat. Their strategic vulnerability comes from command scarcity and coalition dependence, not necessarily a higher absolute future death count.

## Supply

The ten-day portal reserve now declines gradually rather than disappearing at once. Campaign reaches a median minimum supply near 67 per cent and Standard near 81 per cent. Veteran and Desperate normally establish enough captured capacity before the smaller initial force exhausts its reserve.

This is coherent for the current continental abstraction, but disrupted corridors and naval isolation must be stress-tested inside the vertical slice. A Europe-wide median cannot prove that individual player-created salients are safe.

## Pragmatic occupation

Client governments create more resistance and slower campaign completion. Campaign, Standard and Veteran still complete consistently, but generally exceed the easier end of their target ranges. Desperate completes only around 59 per cent of runs within 500 days and has a median completion near 461 days among successes.

Modern formations remain available, but their delayed political reliability creates more failed operations and contemporary casualties. Pragmatic occupation is therefore viable in moderation, not as a cost-free faster option.

## Coercive occupation

Coercive campaigns consistently reach strategic crisis. They produce thousands of refused orders, widespread defections, severe resistance and direct outside intervention.

- Campaign completes only about 22 per cent of runs.
- Standard, Veteran and Desperate do not complete at the median.
- Every coercive setting reaches the nuclear-risk band in most or all runs.

Coerced formations exist and can occasionally fight, but their political unreliability prevents them replacing an accepted coalition. This confirms the desired result without disabling the option artificially.

## Decisions

- Accept the restrained campaign medians as the foundation balance baseline.
- Retain independent modern formations and policy-specific mobilisation thresholds.
- Retain the gradual ten-day portal reserve.
- Retain material support as the minimum full-conquest escalation stage.
- Retain coercive occupation as available but strategically dangerous.
- Carry all major state variables into the playable engine.
- Do not tune individual corridors or dated national forces until the World State and vertical-slice playtests exist.

## Foundation exit decision

The core systems are coherent enough to leave simulation-first development. No remaining mechanic requires another isolated model before implementation begins.

The next work package should merge the current foundation and build the playable regional vertical slice with a real map interface, selectable formations, turn resolution, save and load, basic AI and player-facing forecasts.

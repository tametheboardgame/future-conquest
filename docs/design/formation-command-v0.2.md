# Formation Command v0.2

## Purpose

The first playable slice proved the map, turn loop, occupation model and browser deployment, but its single aggregate army made the campaign almost impossible to lose. Formation Command v0.2 replaces that abstraction with persistent friendly and enemy formations.

## Player formations

The expedition begins as four selectable task groups. Each group has its own:

- location
- personnel strength
- functional and damaged armour
- morale
- local supply
- operational status

A task group can move to an adjacent controlled territory, attack an adjacent enemy territory, recover, or remain as a garrison. Orders are resolved by advancing the campaign day.

## Persistent enemy forces

Each enemy territory begins with a persistent defence command. Enemy personnel, armour, readiness and entrenchment survive between operations. Defeated formations retreat to neighbouring enemy territory when a route exists; otherwise they are destroyed or captured.

Enemy formations reinforce over time. At high escalation, coalition intervention brigades enter the theatre.

## Supply and occupation

Supply now follows a connected route of controlled territories back to the portal. Cut-off territories are isolated. Task groups in isolated territories lose local supply, morale and eventually personnel.

Garrisoned task groups suppress resistance, improve legitimacy and build local fortification. This creates a direct trade-off between concentrating forces for an attack and protecting the territory already occupied.

## Combat consequences

Combat losses are applied to the attacking task group and defending formations rather than a global pool. Casualties are divided into killed and wounded. Wounded personnel enter a medical pool and gradually return to supplied task groups. Damaged armour is repaired over time where supply permits.

Enemy counterattacks target weak frontier territories. Successful counterattacks can recapture territory and force local task groups to retreat.

## Difficulty

Three campaign settings are available:

- Story: weaker enemy formations, fewer counterattacks and faster medical recovery.
- Standard: intended baseline.
- Hard: stronger formations, more counterattacks and slower recovery.

## Deferred work

The next phase should add commander vulnerability, multi-group attacks, explicit reinforcement allocation, deeper political escalation events, improved animation and a larger automated balance harness.

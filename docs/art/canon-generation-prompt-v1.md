# Future Conquest canon generation prompt v1.0

Use this as the shared base instruction for all new visual generation. Add scene-specific composition after it.

## Required base prompt

`Future Conquest visual canon: painted military graphic-novel illustration with realistic human proportions and grounded military design, approximately two centuries beyond the present. Dark, serious, oppressive and disciplined. Technology is advanced but functional, modular, repairable, field-maintainable and constrained by power, heat, damage and logistics. Human operators are not biologically enhanced. Powered armour is physically imposing but agile, with standardised replaceable modules, visible flexible under-suit at joints, matte adaptive smart-skin, multispectral signature management, restrained segmented optics and minimal cyan emission. Armour shows maintenance history through mismatched replacement plates, serials, fasteners, repaired surfaces and repainting. Primary energy rifles remain separate, compact, rugged and deeply suit-linked, with protected power cells and credible heat management. The General is always in modular command armour; any long coat or overlayer is functional battlefield equipment with no ceremonial decoration. Future Europe is inhabited but exhausted, repeatedly repaired and close to collapse. Present-day Europe is recognisable, ordinary and realistically equipped. The temporal portal is a violent spatial failure with an impossible dark centre, pale cyan distortion and limited violet-white instability. Painterly cinematic lighting, strong readable silhouettes, tactile weather and materials, restrained role-based palette, no text embedded in production art unless explicitly requested.`

## Required negative direction

`No biological super-soldiers, no superhero anatomy or poses, no giant shoulders, no walking tanks, no decorative armour, no capes, no medals, no gold trim, no wings, no skulls, no imperial or gothic command styling, no clean magic portal ring, no constant neon lighting, no perfect invisibility, no bubble shields, no standard infantry flight, no standard infantry tunnelling, no oversized cluttered fantasy weapons, no alien or space-opera design, no franchise imitation, no celebrity likeness, no generated logos treated as canon, no embedded captions or watermarks.`

## Reference rule

Always supply the approved relevant image from `reference/` where the generation system supports visual references. A written prompt alone is not sufficient for recurring characters.

## Character continuity rule

For any scene containing the General, specify the selected male-presenting or female-presenting canon variant and provide the armour reference and the relevant section of the approved visual board. The player-provided name is applied in UI or captions, not embedded into illustration source art.

## Production rule

Generate source compositions without text and with space reserved for captions. Where motion-comic parallax is intended, request or separate background, midground, foreground and effects layers. Exact place names and insignia must be omitted unless the scene brief explicitly approves them.

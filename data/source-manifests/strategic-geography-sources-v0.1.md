# Strategic geography sources v0.1

## OurAirports

- Files: `airports.csv` and `runways.csv`
- Retrieved: 2026-08-01
- Update pattern: nightly source dump
- Terms: public-domain dedication; attribution requested but not required
- Use: airport identity, location, source classification, scheduled-service flag
  and longest open runway

## NGA World Port Index

- Dataset: World Port Index point feature service
- Publisher: US National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency
- Retrieved: 2026-08-01
- Terms: public-domain US Government work
- Use: stable port identifier, port name and location

## SimpleMaps via world-cities-json

- Dataset: World Cities Database redistribution
- Terms: CC BY 4.0
- Use: population-based city candidates and national capitals

## Planned sources

- Natural Earth public-domain physical vectors for broad terrain and hydrography
- OpenStreetMap / Geofabrik daily extracts under ODbL 1.0 for strategic transport
  hubs and critical crossings

Raw downloads remain reproducible build inputs and are not committed. Generated
candidate layers retain source identifiers for auditing and refresh comparison.
